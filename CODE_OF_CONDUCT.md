# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful participation in hankoShell Operator, regardless of
background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experiences and viewpoints, and accept corrections.
- Keep collaboration professional and accessible to newcomers.
- Respect people's privacy and their right to decline contact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks,
repeated unwanted contact and disclosure of another person's private
information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@alien6.com](mailto:contact@alien6.com)
with the subject `hankoShell Operator code of conduct`. Include the relevant
context and links; avoid publicly sharing identifying or sensitive material.
Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers review reports confidentially and share information only as needed
to investigate and respond. Responses depend on the circumstances and may
include a correction request, content removal, a warning, or temporary or
permanent exclusion from project spaces. We explain decisions to affected
participants where appropriate. Requests for reconsideration can use the same
contact; a maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome.
